# Queyanlun 缺演论

**An Open Philosophy of Absence, Creation, and Civilizational Evolution**

> Civilization is not the accumulation of answers, but the evolution of what remains absent.

[中文](README.md) · [Rights](RIGHTS.md) · [Citation](CITATION.cff) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md)

## About

Queyanlun begins with the idea of the gap: what is absent, unfinished, or not yet brought into the world. It develops this idea into a philosophical framework for understanding desire, creation, knowledge, and civilizational evolution.

This repository is the public, authoritative source for approved Chinese and English editions. It will host versioned manuscripts, EPUB editions, reading PDFs, and a verifiable publication history.

The project exists for cultural transmission and public thought rather than profit. Complete editions may be read, downloaded, copied, and redistributed without charge under the stated license.
